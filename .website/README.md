FROOPYLAND — Unicorns & Rainbows
A game based on the iconic Rick and Morty episode "The ABC's of Beth" (Season 3, Episode 9)
In the director's cut, you can select levels from the main menu for testing

Beth bursts into the garage: the news say Tommy's dad is being executed TODAY for murder! Rick grabs his portable quantum projector — and the duo heads to Froopyland, a childhood paradise Rick once built with his own hands. He swears: everything here is child-proofed, NOTHING can go wrong. Of course it can.

On the way to the prison they face SEVEN trials — one for every rainbow color:

🔴 Garage — the projector is fried: flip cable segments, zero every pipe and open the chalk door.
🟠 Nest — a giant winged pony kidnaps Rick into its nest! Fight off waves of hungry baby unicorns before Rick becomes dinner.
🟡 Memory Gate — Tommy warded the path: copy five light sequences in a row. One mistake — back to square one.
🟢 Cells — collect five memory chips and bring them to Rick one at a time while froupies chase you.
🔵 Trace — a chalk lock: trace the zigzag of dots without lifting your line to draw the counter-door.
🟣 Tommy — a knife duel: grab the pink arrow, aim, and land three hits.
🌈 DNA — solve a 6×12 cable puzzle connecting the top-left corner to the bottom-right — can Rick clone Tommy before the execution?

All of this fits in 13 KB of vanilla JavaScript: no assets — every character, sound and location is drawn procedurally in code. Mouse or touchscreen only. In true Rick and Morty fashion: dark humor, burps, wubba lubba dub dub and amoral genius solutions.

Trial seven. Color seven. Save Tommy's dad — or at least save yourself.
